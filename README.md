# Ideator-Credit-System
Designed a gamified credit economy for PitchSap, defining reward mechanisms, redemption strategies, engagement incentives, and premium unlock systems.

## Overview

The Ideator Credit System is a gamified engagement and rewards framework designed for PitchSap to encourage meaningful user participation and improve long-term platform retention.

The system enables ideators to earn credits through valuable platform activities such as referrals, community engagement, idea submissions, and subscription milestones. These credits can then be redeemed for premium platform features, creating an alternative to direct monetary purchases while increasing user engagement and platform activity.

---

## Problem Statement

Many users become inactive after onboarding due to limited incentives for continuous engagement. Additionally, premium platform features often remain inaccessible to users who are unwilling or unable to make direct payments.

The challenge was to design a sustainable reward system that:

- Encourages consistent platform engagement
- Rewards meaningful user contributions
- Increases user retention
- Promotes community participation
- Provides alternative access to premium features
- Supports platform growth through referrals and engagement

---

## Proposed Solution

Designed a platform-wide credit economy where users earn credits by completing valuable platform activities and redeem them for premium benefits.

The proposed system introduces a balanced reward mechanism that aligns user incentives with business objectives while encouraging recurring participation and community growth.

---

## Ways to Earn Credits

The proposed earning model includes rewards for:

- Referring new users to the platform
- Successful referral conversions
- Posting about PitchSap on social media
- Providing valuable consultant feedback
- Community posts and comments
- High-engagement community content
- Daily login streaks
- First idea submission
- First AI review completion
- First consultancy booking
- Subscription renewal bonuses
- Monthly leaderboard rewards
- Launchpad rankings

---

## Ways to Redeem Credits

Users can redeem earned credits for premium platform benefits including:

- Additional AI Reviews
- Bundle AI Review Packages
- Extra Idea Submissions
- Premium Library Templates
- Pitch Deck Reviews
- Consultant Discount Coupons
- Launchpad Visibility Boosts
- Priority Idea Review Queue
- Premium Event & Workshop Discounts

---

## Product Thinking

The credit system was designed to create a sustainable engagement loop by rewarding high-value user actions rather than passive activity.

The reward structure focuses on:

- Increasing user retention
- Encouraging platform referrals
- Improving community engagement
- Supporting premium feature adoption
- Driving recurring platform usage
- Creating a scalable gamification framework

The proposed redemption model also balances user value with business sustainability by assigning appropriate credit costs based on feature value and platform impact.
